WWN punishes "everyone attack head-on". Here are the basics that work in any theatre.

## 1. Combat recon

Before an offensive, send a cheap unit to see where turrets and AA stand.

- If the scout dies — you already know where the defense is.
- If it reaches the center — the enemy has no front.

## 2. Focus fire

Concentrate damage on one target: 10 units shooting different targets are worse than 10 units shooting one.

> Rule: **a dead unit is useless, a wounded one is dangerous**.

## 3. Pincer

Split your army into two groups:

1. The main group attacks the front.
2. The mobile group flanks or takes an empty route.

Works best against positional defense.

## 4. Siege

Order against a fortified position:

1. Artillery breaks **turrets**.
2. Line units enter and soak damage.
3. AA cover guards against aircraft.
4. Saboteurs cut power — the defense collapses on its own.

## 5. Retreating

Don't fear retreating. A saved army beats a beautiful win:

- fall back to your own AA;
- lure the enemy under turrets;
- switch theatres: leave the ground, strike from orbit.

## Counter table

| Against | Answer with |
| --- | --- |
| Air | AA, interceptors |
| Heavy ships | Torpedoes, swarms of small units |
| Artillery | Mobile raids, aircraft |
| Stealth | Radars, detection |
| Economic spam | Raids on mining |

## Training

Take a replay of your defeat and answer three questions:

1. Where did I lose my first unit?
2. What did I fail to scout?
3. In which theatre did I lose the game?
