Macro in WWN is the art of turning quiet minutes into an advantage. Most matches are lost not in combat but in the first five minutes, when a player builds "almost the right thing".

## Opening: the first 90 seconds

**Goal:** two harvesters + a power plant + a scout.

1. Harvesters to the nearest resources.
2. Power plant right after the first harvester.
3. Scout along a route through the map center.

Don't build barracks "just in case": an idle military building is a stolen harvester.

## Expansion

- **Second base:** as soon as the first one runs at full mining.
- **Third point:** only with protection — turrets and AA.
- **Stretch rule:** each new point shouldn't lengthen your defense line by more than 20%.

## Production

Keep **at least two factories**:

- one for the current army;
- one to replace losses.

If you build an army "on the last money", the first failed attack becomes fatal.

## Power

| Situation | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Lights flicker while building | Power plant, immediately |
| Lots of shields and radars | Build a backup plant |
| Enemy raids your rear | Spread plants across the map |

## Timings (reference)

- **0:30** — first harvester.
- **1:30** — power plant + scout.
- **3:00** — second mining point.
- **6:00** — two factories, backup power.
- **10:00** — either a fleet or a siege corps.

## Exercise

Start a match against an easy AI and try to build **no combat units** for eight minutes. You'll be surprised how many resources pile up by minute nine — and how fast they turn into an army.
