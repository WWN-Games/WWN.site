WWN grows through its community: balance, maps, models, writing and lore. Here is how to help.

## How to contribute

1. Fork the [WWN-Mod repository](https://gitlab.com/wwn.games/wwn.mod-rustedwarfare/WWN-Mod) on GitLab.
2. Create a separate branch for your feature or fix.
3. Make the changes and describe them in your commit.
4. Open a merge request with a detailed description: what changed and why.

## What is needed most

- **Balance** — unit testing, number proposals, replays of problematic matches.
- **Maps** — new scenarios for casual play and the league.
- **Lore & writing** — edits, translations, wiki article additions.
- **Bug reports** — detailed issue descriptions on the [Issues page](https://gitlab.com/wwn.games/wwn.mod-rustedwarfare/WWN-Mod/-/issues).

## Good habits

<div class="callout">
<strong>One task — one merge request.</strong> It makes changes easier to review and faster to accept.
</div>

- Attach screenshots or replays to balance changes.
- For maps, specify size, type and player count.
- For writing, keep the WWN style and the terminology from the wiki.

## Discussion

Join the [WWN Discord server](https://discord.gg/sRwKDGrbDy): balance talks, new build tests and playtest groups live there.
