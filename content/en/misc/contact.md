We always try to stay in touch with players. We are happy to help you and will do our best to sort out your problem.

## Community

- **Discord** — [join the WWN server](https://discord.gg/sRwKDGrbDy): mod talk, balance and matches.
- **GitHub** — [report a bug](https://github.com/WWN-Games/WWN.issues/issues/new/choose).

## How to report a problem

Found a bug? You can easily report it on our [GitHub](https://github.com/WWN-Games/WWN.issues/issues/new/choose) by filling in a simple form. You can also tell us about the bug in our [Discord](https://discord.gg/sRwKDGrbDy).
