Questions, bugs, ideas and finding opponents — it all lives in the WWN community.

## Community

- **Discord** — [join the WWN server](https://discord.gg/sRwKDGrbDy): mod talk, balance and matches.
- **Steam Workshop** — the [mod page](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3247893564). Subscription, updates and comments.

## How to report a problem

1. Make sure you run the **current mod version**.
2. Describe the issue: what happened, on which map, with which actions.
3. Attach a screenshot or replay if possible.
4. Report the issue in [Discord](https://discord.gg/sRwKDGrbDy).
