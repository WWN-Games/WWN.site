> **Draft.** A template about how the two theatres interact.

The key mechanic of WWN is **two theatres of war** that empower each other. A player who links orbit and surface beats any "one-sided" opponent.

## Space

**Fleet tasks:**

- Cut enemy supply routes.
- Support ground forces with orbital strikes.
- Protect your own docks and shipyards.

**Traits:**

- Ships are expensive and slow to replace: losing a squadron costs minutes of production.
- Position beats speed: a fleet in ambush is stronger than a fleet in the open.

## Surface

**Army tasks:**

- Hold resource points and cities.
- Build and defend factories.
- Siege fortifications once orbit is clear.

**Traits:**

- Cheap units and constant pressure.
- Ground forces can't reach orbit early — they need AA systems.

## Three interaction patterns

1. **Orbit presses, ground secures.** The fleet wipes out defenses — the army takes the ruins.
2. **Ground distracts, space strikes.** A ground attack pulls forces to the front — the fleet enters empty orbit.
3. **Double blockade.** Cut both orbital and ground routes: the enemy loses army and resources alike.

## The main rule

> Don't split the war into two independent battles. One theatre without the other is half an army.
