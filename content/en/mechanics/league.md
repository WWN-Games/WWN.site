**WWN League** is the mod's competitive format: fixed maps, transparent rules and a seasonal player rating.

## Season format

- **Duration:** a season runs for 3 months, then playoffs.
- **Matches:** 1v1, Bo3 in groups and Bo5 in playoffs.
- **Rating:** points for wins, bonus points for clean victories.
- **Divisions:** open, pro and elite.

## Rules

1. Both sides must run the **current mod version**.
2. Maps are picked from the **approved season pool**.
3. Exploits, bug abuse and any third-party unit modifications are banned.
4. Disputes are resolved by league admins; decisions are published in the community.
5. Matches can be reviewed by recording or replay.

## Schedule

| Stage | When | Format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sign-ups | Week 1 of the season | Online form |
| Group stage | Weeks 2–8 | Bo3 |
| Playoffs | Weeks 9–11 | Bo5 |
| Final | Week 12 | Bo7, streamed |

## How to join

1. Join the WWN community (Discord).
2. Register in the season thread.
3. Prepare the current mod version and check your maps.
