The economy in WWN isn't a backdrop for war — it is the war. The match goes to whoever builds a stable chain of "mining → production → supply" first.

## Three pillars

### 1. Mining

- Every harvester brings in resources while it lives.
- Mining points must be **defended**, not just captured.
- Expansion is always more dangerous than it looks: a stretched economy is a gift to raiders.

### 2. Power

- Power plants feed shields, radars and heavy factories.
- Overload the grid and the whole defense goes dark.
- Keep **backup plants** in the rear.

### 3. Logistics

- Space is your road network. Whoever holds orbit decides what reaches the front.
- A blockaded route doesn't kill a base instantly — it kills it three minutes later.

## Spending balance

A healthy macro benchmark:

| Direction | Share of income |
| --- | --- |
| Economy expansion | 40% |
| Army | 40% |
| Base defense | 20% |

Spend more than 60% on the army without attacking and you lose tempo. Spend less than 30% and you lose the map.

## Checkpoints

- **3 minutes:** second mining base.
- **6 minutes:** backup power plant and first AA.
- **10 minutes:** a fleet or a siege corps depending on your plan.

## Playing against greedy macro

If the enemy invests in economy, don't give them three quiet minutes: raid, sabotage, blockade routes. Economies love silence.
