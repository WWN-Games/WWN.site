WWN changes classic Rusted Warfare in three dimensions: **scale**, **economy** and **tempo**. Here is what to know before your first game.

## Two theatres of war

The mod's signature mechanic: combat happens in **space** and on the **surface** at the same time.

- Orbital fleets control supply routes and strike the surface.
- Ground armies protect the economy and capture points.
- Losing orbit makes ground defense pointless — and vice versa.

## Economy and power

Classic resources are complemented by energy:

- **Resources** — mining, construction, army.
- **Power** — shields, radars, heavy factories.
- Destroying power plants shuts down defense across the whole base.

## Tech branches

Each faction develops its own tech tree. The usual fork:

1. **Aggression** — fast units and early war.
2. **Defense** — shields, AA, fortifications.
3. **Fleet** — heavy ships and orbital control.

## Game tempo

An average 1v1 match lasts **15–25 minutes**. That's faster than classic RTS, so mistakes cost more and recon is worth more.
