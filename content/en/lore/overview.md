WWN is a large-scale modification for **Rusted Warfare** that turns a single battlefield into a galaxy at war. Its campaigns follow two factions — the [Fenearth Republic](fenearth.md) and the [Proton Movement](proton.md) — through the Triple Conflict, but their struggle is only the latest chapter of a history that stretches back millions of years.

![Map of the WWN galaxy](../assets/img/lore/dp-en.webp)

## A galaxy under quarantine

Long ago, a race of giants known as the **Wanderers** travelled between galaxies, building gate systems and studying every world and species they could find. Their exploration ended when they entered a completely dead galaxy filled with biomass: it was the home of a virus of **bio-nanites**, half of which existed in the fourth dimension. The nanites could take control of any living being and transform it into more biomass. Unaware of the danger, the Wanderers infected themselves and part of the known galaxies.

After unsuccessful attempts to find a cure, the Wanderers quarantined the infected galaxies to prevent further spread. To guard the gate systems and continue the search for a cure, they created the **Keepers** — a synthetic race bred from the most resistant fungus-like species. One of the quarantined galaxies was the Milky Way, which the Wanderers had only recently discovered and had not fully explored or equipped with gates.

## The Keepers and the nanites

Over time, the Keepers discovered infections caused by mutated nanites. They tried to reduce the nanites' numbers and strength by eliminating all infected beings, earning widespread hatred in the process. When the Wanderers never made contact again and the nanites seemed to be suppressed, the Keepers declined and deviated from their purpose. In the eyes of most races they became mere mad fungi, ready to destroy others under the single pretext of "Purification."

## The modern galaxy

Centuries later, the galaxy had survived the War of Purification, the reform of the Star Confederation into the Galactic Federation, the Galactic Turmoil and the rise of new states. Then, in 2121 PZLI, Minel attacked Fenzem, Proton struck back, and the Triple Conflict began. This is the war in which the events of the mod take place — the war you play through as the [Fenearth Republic](fenearth.md) or the [Proton Movement](proton.md).
