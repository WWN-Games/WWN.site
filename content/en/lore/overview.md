**WWN** is a mod for **Rusted Warfare** set in the Milky Way galaxy. Two playable factions clash here — the Fenearth Republic and the Proton Movement — and the events of the mod unfold against the backdrop of the Triple Conflict, which began in 2121 PZLI.

## The beginning

Long ago there was a race of giants striving to explore their surroundings. They were called the **Wanderers**. They could travel between galaxies, creating gate systems in them. Their goal was to study as many galaxies and species in them as possible.

One day the Wanderers stumbled upon a completely dead galaxy filled with biomass — it turned out to be a virus of bio-nanites, half of which reside in the fourth dimension. The nanites can take control of any living being and turn it into biomass. Unaware of this, the Wanderers infected themselves and part of the known galaxies.

After unsuccessful attempts to find an antidote, they decided to quarantine all galaxies to prevent the infection from spreading. To do this, they created a synthetic race of **Keepers** from the fungus-like species most resistant to the nanites. The Keepers were to guard the gate systems in the quarantined galaxies and also try to find an antidote on their own.

## Map of the galaxy

One of the quarantined galaxies was the Milky Way — recently discovered by the Wanderers and not yet fully built up with gates. This is where the entire subsequent history of WWN unfolds.

## Factions and races

- **Fenearth Republic (FR/Fenearth)** — an independent state formed during the Galactic Turmoil in 2061 PZLI.
- **Proton Movement** — a union of clans and small states on a federative basis in the Oten Territory; the main race is the Vasst (Protonians).
