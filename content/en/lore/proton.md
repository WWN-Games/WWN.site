The **Proton Movement** (Unified Oten PHATHOU) is one of the two factions of WWN — an independent sovereign state and a union of clans and small states that controls the Oten Territory.

![Emblem of the Proton Movement](../assets/img/factions/faction-proton.svg)

## General Information

The Proton Movement (Unified Oten PHATHOU) is an independent sovereign state, a union of clans and small states on a federative basis, located in the Oten Territory in the Capo-Bul sector of the galaxy and occupying 22% of the galaxy. Despite the presence of a large number of subjects, it is an extremely unitary and centralized state where the clan and federative components are more of a formality and a tribute to history. Clans and other subjects retain only a small degree of autonomy and have significant historical and cultural differences among themselves. Each clan has its own traditions and charter, and various races and their subspecies with different rights reside on their territories. Each clan has its own troops, which operate as part of the United Proton Liberation Army. The government consists of elected representatives from all subjects, with the greatest weight given to the ancestral clan Kipfus. Governance is carried out locally by clans, which in turn unquestioningly obey Kipfus and the capital on the planet Gamprellaka. The main races are Vastt (Protonians), Syndigates, Unorons, Okands. The main currency is Energy Credits.

## History of Origin

The movement was formed during the Galactic Turmoil in 2020 PZLI by the Vastt clan Kipfus (KHEFOUS) as a national liberation movement of the Vastt and all sympathizers from the control of the declining and failing Galactic Federation and the impending threat from the newly formed Minel (the main historical enemy of the Oten Territory). In its region, it became a mass movement and united many races, and thanks to the Gate System, Proton spread with incredible speed across the entire Oten Territory.

### Name

Proton (PHATHOU) is a name where PHA in Unified Oten means "I" or "One," and THOU means "order," literally translating to "I order" or "First order" and symbolizing that order will reign for the first time in a long time on the Oten Territory and that everyone brings this order. However, due to a misinterpretation by Minel propaganda, which portrayed the Protonians and Vastt (the main race in the movement) as evil, they became known as Protonians. Protonians are very warlike and diverse in traditions and charters, and internal conflicts periodically arise, which can escalate into skirmishes until the government intervenes. However, Protonians have always united against common enemies.

## Technology and Armament

For a long time, like the rest of the galaxy, the Proton Movement used old Galactic Federation equipment. At the same time, in hundreds of backyard workshops, new prototypes of the most unusual and often ridiculous weapons were created. However, they were immediately tested in battle, and the best ones were adopted by the army and replaced the lost "ordinary" equipment. Currently, Proton has developed all necessary types of weapons and civilian equipment and has almost completely replaced everything left from the Galactic Federation. Each clan has its own production system, albeit connected to others, and each enterprise uses its own materials and technologies. Therefore, their equipment may look different. All equipment has a simple design, making it very rugged and reliable but fragile. This is compensated by widespread field repairs and available materials and manpower. Although Protonians use what they consider a very "safe and cautious" tactic, they usually have more casualties than others.
