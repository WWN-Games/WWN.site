The **Proton Movement (Unified Oten PHATHOU)** is an independent sovereign state, a union of clans and small states on a federative basis, located in the Oten Territory in the Capo-Bul sector of the galaxy.

## General information

The Proton Movement occupies 22% of the galaxy. Despite having a huge number of subjects, it is an extremely unitary and centralized state where the clan and federative components are more of a formality and a tribute to history. Clans and other subjects retain only a small degree of autonomy and have great historical and cultural differences among themselves. Each clan has its own traditions and charter, and various races and their subspecies with different rights reside on its territory. Each clan has its own troops, which operate as part of the United Proton Liberation Army. The government consists of elected representatives from all subjects, with the greatest weight given to the ancestral clan Kipfus. Governance is carried out locally by clans, which in turn unquestioningly obey Kipfus and the capital on the planet Gamprellaka. The main races are Vasst (Protonians), Syndigates, Unorons, Okands. The main currency is Energy Credits.

## History of origin

The movement was formed during the Galactic Turmoil in 2020 PZLI by the Vasst clan Kipfus (KHEFOUS) as a national liberation movement of the Vasst and all sympathizers from the control of the hopeless, fading Galactic Federation and the looming threat from the newly formed Minel (the main historical enemy of the Oten Territory). In its region it became a mass movement and united many races, and thanks to the Gate System, Proton spread across the entire Oten Territory with incredible speed.

### Name

Proton (PHATHOU) is a name where PHA in Unified Oten means "I" or "One", and THOU means "order"; it literally sounds like "I am order" or "First order" and symbolizes that for the first time in a long while order will reign in the Oten Territory and that everyone brings this order. However, due to a misinterpretation by Minel propaganda, which portrayed the Protonians and the Vasst, who were the main race in the movement, as evil, the name Protonians stuck to them. Protonians are very warlike and quite diverse in traditions and charters, and conflicts periodically arise within, which can escalate into skirmishes until the government intervenes. However, Protonians have always united against common enemies.

## Technology and armament

For a long time, like the whole galaxy, the Proton Movement used the old equipment of the Galactic Federation. But at the same time, in hundreds of backyard workshops, new prototypes of the most unusual and often ridiculous weapons were built on their knees. However, they were immediately tested in battle, and the best of them entered service in the army and replaced the lost "ordinary" equipment. At the moment, Proton has developed all the necessary types of weapons and civilian equipment and has almost completely replaced everything left from the Galactic Federation. Each clan has its own production system, albeit connected with the others, and each enterprise uses its own materials and technologies. Therefore, their equipment may look different. All equipment has a simple design, which makes it very unpretentious and reliable, but fragile. This is compensated by widespread field repairs and available materials and manpower. Although the Protonians use what they consider a very "safe and cautious" tactic, they usually have more casualties than others.
