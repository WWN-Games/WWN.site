In WWN every unit has a role, and the winner isn't the one with the "stronger unit" but the one with the right composition. Below are the basic classes.

## Unit classes

### Recon

Cheap, fast units that reveal the map and track movement.

- They cost pennies and die instantly.
- Main rule: **scouts are expendable**.

### Line units

Tanks, cruisers, infantry — what holds the front and takes damage.

- The backbone of any army.
- Need cover against dedicated counters.

### Artillery and siege

Long-range fire and demolition of fortifications.

- They don't survive without cover.
- They decide long assaults.

### AA and air defense

Protection from air and space.

- Mandatory near factories and cities.
- Useless against ground vehicles.

### Support and heroes

Repairs, buffs, shields, precise strikes.

- They amplify regular units.
- Often decide one-on-one duels.

## How to read the stats

| Stat | Meaning | Affects |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cost | Production price | Army tempo |
| Health | Hull HP | How much damage it takes |
| Damage | Damage per second | Trade speed |
| Speed | Movement | Mobility, disengaging |
| Range | Attack distance | Who starts the fight |

## The rule of three

1. Someone **takes damage**.
2. Someone **deals damage**.
3. Someone **sees the map**.

If your composition lacks any of the three — it's unbalanced.
