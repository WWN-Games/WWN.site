A base in WWN isn't a set of buildings — it's a living organism: the economy feeds production, production protects the economy. Breaking this chain usually means defeat.

## Building categories

### Economy

- **Harvesters** — bring in resources.
- **Power plants** — power shields, radars and heavy factories.
- **Storage** — raises storage caps.

### Production

- **War factory** — ground vehicles.
- **Orbital dock** — spaceships.
- **Air factory** — aircraft.

### Defense

- **AA turrets** — protection from air and space.
- **Shield generator** — absorbs area damage.
- **Radar** — vision and stealth detection.

### Headquarters

The **Command Center** is the heart of the base. It unlocks tech, defines available buildings and is almost always the enemy's main target.

## Defense layout

<div class="callout">
<strong>Base rule:</strong> every important object should sit inside AA range, and every turret cluster should be under radar vision.
</div>

1. **Outer ring** — turrets and minefields.
2. **Middle ring** — AA and shields.
3. **Inner ring** — the core: HQ, factories, power plants.

## Common mistakes

- The whole economy in one spot — one raid and the match is over.
- No radars — stealth units run wild in your rear.
- No AA near factories — air power erases production in a minute.

## Pre-attack checklist

- [ ] Recon showed the enemy base layout.
- [ ] You have siege artillery.
- [ ] AA cover is assembled.
- [ ] All approach routes are known.
