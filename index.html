Mod features

What WWN adds to Rusted Warfare

Space battles

A new mechanic: fights are no longer limited to one planet. Orbital fleets cut supply lines and support the army from above.

New units

Fenearth and Proton hardware: from support ships to improvised Proton prototypes proven in battle.

New maps

Space and mixed maps with varied environments — from orbital corridors to fortified planetary bases.

Improved gameplay

Reworked mod mechanics: economy, defense and sieges. Every match plays out differently.

Lore & factions

A full universe: galaxy history, two powers, races and era maps — all in the wiki.

A living community

Updates, balance talks, custom maps and matches. Join the Discord and play with others.

How to install

Download the mod

Subscribe on Steam Workshop, download the archive from Google Drive, or grab the latest GitLab release.

Install

Steam enables it automatically. For the archive, unpack it into the Rusted Warfare mods/units directory.

Play

Launch Rusted Warfare, enable the mod in the mods menu and host a game on WWN maps.

Requires a legit copy of Rusted Warfare (Steam / Google Play). The mod is free. Latest releases are also published on GitLab
