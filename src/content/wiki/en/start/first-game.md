Your first WWN match can be overwhelming: two theatres, two factions and an unfamiliar economy. This guide walks you through the first fifteen minutes.

## Step 0. Setup

1. Install the mod from **Steam Workshop** or **Google Drive** (see the buttons on the home page).
2. Launch Rusted Warfare and enable WWN in the mods menu.
3. For your first game pick a **beginner map** and an easy AI.

## Step 1. The first minutes

- Build two **harvesters** and send them to the nearest resources.
- Build a **power plant** — no power means no shields and no factories.
- Send a **scout** around the map: knowing the map beats your first vehicle.

## Step 2. Defense without panic

- Place **AA** next to your mining: aircraft are the first pressure tool.
- Don't chase a perfect base: in WWN the first attack arrives fast.

<div class="callout">
<strong>Beginner rule:</strong> if you don't know what to do — build harvesters and recon. It's almost never a mistake.
</div>

## Step 3. Your first army

Pick one plan and stick to it:

1. **Aggression:** 4–6 line units → hit the nearest enemy point.
2. **Defense:** 2–3 defense lines → artillery → counter-attack.
3. **Fleet:** dock → 2 cruisers → orbit control.

## Common beginner mistakes

| Mistake | Consequence | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No recon | Attacking into the fog | One scout every 2 minutes |
| Economy in one place | One raid = defeat | Spread mining and power |
| No AA | Air erases the base | AA near every factory |
| Ground-only play | Orbit decides the game | Build a dock and a fleet |
